Layout primitive that horizontally centers content and constrains it to a maximum width. Wraps page-level sections, navigation bars, or content columns; not a general-purpose box.

## Variants

**default** — standard max-width for page sections and general content areas.

**nav** — same max-width as default, with compact vertical padding for navigation bars.

**blog** — narrower max-width for long-form reading content such as article bodies.
