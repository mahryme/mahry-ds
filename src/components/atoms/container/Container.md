Full-width layout wrapper that sets a section's horizontal page gutter and, in the default variant, its vertical padding. Content is placed inside it; a section holds one Container. On desktop, content is capped at 1360 and the container is centred once the viewport exceeds 1440. Carries no background of its own.

## Variants

**default** — adds vertical padding on top of the horizontal gutter; for a section that stands alone, with no adjacent band supplying its top and bottom spacing, e.g. the home section, which holds its own header.

**no-py** — horizontal gutter only, no vertical padding; for a section that sits between bands that already supply vertical spacing, e.g. the header and footer on the article and cases pages. Content starts at the container's top edge.
