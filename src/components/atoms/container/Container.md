Full-width layout wrapper that sets a section's horizontal page gutter and vertical padding. Content is placed inside it; a section holds one Container. On desktop, content is capped at 1376 and the container is centred once the viewport exceeds 1440. Carries no background of its own.

## Variants

**default** — adds vertical padding on top of the horizontal gutter; for a section that stands alone, with no adjacent band supplying its top and bottom spacing, e.g. the home section, which holds its own header.

**no-pt** — horizontal gutter and bottom padding, no top padding; for an internal page reached from elsewhere, where the header and breadcrumbs already set the top spacing, e.g. the case study and article pages. Content starts at the container's top edge.
